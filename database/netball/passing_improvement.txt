NETBALL PASSING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Passing is the foundation of netball and allows teams to move the ball efficiently across the court. Accurate passing helps create scoring opportunities and maintain possession.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn chest pass and shoulder pass techniques.
•	Focus on passing accuracy.
•	Practice proper hand positioning.
•	Develop communication with teammates.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve passing speed and timing.
•	Learn bounce and overhead passes.
•	Develop court awareness.
•	Practice passing under pressure.
Advanced Level:
•	Execute accurate passes in tight spaces.
•	Anticipate teammates' movements.
•	Read defensive positioning.
•	Control game tempo through passing.
Training Recommendations:
•	Passing drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Partner passing: 100 passes per session.
•	Movement-based drills: 3 sessions per week.
•	Match simulation exercises: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Inaccurate passing.
•	Holding the ball too long.
•	Poor communication.
•	Weak passing technique.
Career Guidance:
Strong passing skills are essential for all netball positions and support progression through competitive levels.
