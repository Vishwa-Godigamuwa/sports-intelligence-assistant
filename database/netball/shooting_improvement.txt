NETBALL SHOOTING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Shooting is the skill of scoring goals and is essential for attacking success. Effective shooters combine accuracy, confidence, and proper technique.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn correct shooting technique.
•	Focus on balance and alignment.
•	Practice close-range shooting.
•	Develop consistent release mechanics.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve shooting accuracy from different positions.
•	Practice shooting under pressure.
•	Develop confidence during matches.
•	Improve consistency.
Advanced Level:
•	Maintain high shooting percentages.
•	Perform effectively under pressure.
•	Adapt to defensive pressure.
•	Develop strong game awareness.
Training Recommendations:
•	Shooting drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Goal shooting: 100 attempts per session.
•	Match simulation drills: 2 sessions per week.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor shooting form.
•	Rushing shots.
•	Lack of focus.
•	Inconsistent release.
Career Guidance:
Consistent shooting can establish players as reliable scorers and open opportunities at higher competitive levels.
