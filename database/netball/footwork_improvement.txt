NETBALL FOOTWORK IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Footwork is a crucial netball skill that helps players move efficiently, maintain balance, and avoid stepping violations while creating space on the court.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn correct landing techniques.
•	Practice balanced movement.
•	Develop spatial awareness.
•	Understand footwork rules.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve directional changes.
•	Increase movement efficiency.
•	Practice quick pivots.
•	Enhance balance under pressure.
Advanced Level:
•	Create space effectively.
•	Anticipate play development.
•	Combine movement with tactical awareness.
•	Maintain proper footwork during high-intensity play.
Training Recommendations:
•	Footwork drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Agility ladder exercises: 15 minutes daily.
•	Pivot drills: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Endurance training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Stepping violations.
•	Poor balance.
•	Slow directional changes.
•	Incorrect landing technique.
Career Guidance:
Excellent footwork improves overall netball performance and supports long-term development in school, club, regional, and national competitions.
