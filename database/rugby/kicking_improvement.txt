RUGBY KICKING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Kicking is an important rugby skill used for gaining territory, scoring points, and creating tactical advantages. Effective kicking requires accuracy, power, and decision-making.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper kicking technique.
•	Focus on clean ball contact.
•	Practice short-distance kicks.
•	Develop balance during kicking.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve kicking distance and accuracy.
•	Practice tactical kicks.
•	Develop consistency under pressure.
•	Learn different kicking styles.
Advanced Level:
•	Master goal kicking and field kicking techniques.
•	Adapt kicking strategies to match situations.
•	Target specific field areas accurately.
•	Perform consistently in high-pressure moments.
Training Recommendations:
•	Kicking drills: 4 sessions per week.
•	Target kicking practice: 50 kicks per session.
•	Strength and flexibility training: 3 sessions per week.
•	Match simulation exercises: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor ball contact.
•	Inconsistent technique.
•	Rushing kicks.
•	Poor body balance.
Career Guidance:
Strong kicking abilities increase a player's tactical value and can support advancement through school, club, regional, and professional rugby competitions.
