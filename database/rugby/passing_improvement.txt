RUGBY PASSING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Passing is a fundamental rugby skill that allows teams to move the ball effectively and create attacking opportunities. Accurate passing improves teamwork and ball retention.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper passing grip.
•	Practice basic lateral passes.
•	Focus on passing accuracy.
•	Develop communication with teammates.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve passing speed.
•	Practice passing while running.
•	Develop passing with both hands.
•	Increase awareness of team positioning.
Advanced Level:
•	Execute accurate passes under pressure.
•	Create attacking opportunities with quick distribution.
•	Read defensive formations.
•	Control game tempo through passing.
Training Recommendations:
•	Passing drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Partner passing: 100 passes per session.
•	Small-sided games: 2-3 sessions per week.
•	Agility training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Forward passing.
•	Poor accuracy.
•	Weak communication.
•	Looking only at the receiver.
Career Guidance:
Strong passing skills are essential for all rugby players and support progression through competitive levels.
