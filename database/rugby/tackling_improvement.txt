RUGBY TACKLING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Tackling is a defensive skill used to stop opponents from advancing. Safe and effective tackling requires proper technique, timing, and confidence.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn correct tackling posture.
•	Focus on shoulder contact technique.
•	Keep your head in a safe position.
•	Practice controlled tackling drills.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve timing and positioning.
•	Practice tackling moving opponents.
•	Develop one-on-one tackling confidence.
•	Increase physical strength and stability.
Advanced Level:
•	Execute tackles effectively under pressure.
•	Read attackers' movements.
•	Prevent offloads after tackles.
•	Lead defensive efforts.
Training Recommendations:
•	Tackling drills: 4 sessions per week.
•	Contact training: 2-3 sessions per week.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
•	Match simulation exercises: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor body position.
•	Tackling too high.
•	Closing eyes on contact.
•	Poor timing.
Career Guidance:
Reliable tackling skills are highly valued and contribute significantly to team performance and player development.
