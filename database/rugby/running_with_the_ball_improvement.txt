RUGBY RUNNING WITH THE BALL IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Running with the ball is a key attacking skill in rugby. Effective ball carriers gain territory, break defensive lines, and create scoring opportunities.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper ball-carrying technique.
•	Keep the ball secure in contact situations.
•	Practice straight running lines.
•	Develop balance while carrying the ball.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve speed and agility.
•	Practice side-stepping defenders.
•	Develop evasive running skills.
•	Increase awareness of support players.
Advanced Level:
•	Break defensive lines consistently.
•	Read defensive structures.
•	Maintain control during contact.
•	Make effective decisions while attacking.
Training Recommendations:
•	Running drills: 4 sessions per week.
•	Agility exercises: 3 sessions per week.
•	Ball-carrying drills: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Carrying the ball loosely.
•	Running into pressure unnecessarily.
•	Poor decision-making.
•	Lack of support awareness.
Career Guidance:
Strong ball-carrying ability can help players become effective attackers and advance through competitive rugby pathways.
