ACCELERATION IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Acceleration is the ability to increase speed rapidly after leaving the starting blocks. Effective acceleration helps sprinters reach maximum velocity quickly.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn correct sprinting posture.
•	Focus on powerful leg drive.
•	Practice short-distance sprints.
•	Develop coordinated arm action.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve stride power.
•	Increase acceleration efficiency.
•	Strengthen lower-body muscles.
•	Develop explosive movement patterns.
Advanced Level:
•	Maximize force production.
•	Optimize stride length and frequency.
•	Improve acceleration mechanics.
•	Maintain efficiency during transition phases.
Training Recommendations:
•	Acceleration drills: 3-4 sessions per week.
•	Hill sprints: 6-10 repetitions per session.
•	Sled pushes: 2 sessions per week.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Overstriding.
•	Poor arm action.
•	Standing upright too early.
•	Weak leg drive.
Career Guidance:
Effective acceleration is a key component of sprint success and is essential for competitive performance.
