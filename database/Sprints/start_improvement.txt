START TECHNIQUE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
The sprint start is critical in 100m and 200m races. A powerful and explosive start helps athletes gain an early advantage and build momentum quickly.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn correct starting block setup.
•	Practice the "On Your Marks" and "Set" positions.
•	Focus on explosive movement at the starting signal.
•	Develop balance and body control.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve reaction time.
•	Generate more power from the blocks.
•	Maintain proper body angle during acceleration.
•	Develop consistency in race starts.
Advanced Level:
•	Maximize explosive power.
•	Perfect block clearance technique.
•	Improve reaction under competition pressure.
•	Optimize the transition into acceleration.
Training Recommendations:
•	Start drills: 3-4 sessions per week.
•	Block start practice: 10-15 repetitions per session.
•	Reaction drills: 15 minutes per session.
•	Plyometric training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Rising too quickly.
•	Poor block positioning.
•	Slow reaction to the starter's signal.
•	Overstriding out of the blocks.
Career Guidance:
Strong starting ability is essential for success in sprint events and can significantly improve overall race performance.
