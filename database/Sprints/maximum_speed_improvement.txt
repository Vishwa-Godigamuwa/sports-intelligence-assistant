MAXIMUM SPEED IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Maximum speed refers to the highest velocity a sprinter can achieve during a race. Developing top-end speed is crucial for success in both 100m and 200m events.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper sprint mechanics.
•	Focus on body posture.
•	Maintain relaxed upper-body movement.
•	Practice short sprint intervals.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve stride frequency.
•	Increase stride efficiency.
•	Develop stronger running mechanics.
•	Enhance coordination and balance.
Advanced Level:
•	Maximize sprint efficiency.
•	Maintain relaxation at high speeds.
•	Improve neuromuscular performance.
•	Optimize running technique under pressure.
Training Recommendations:
•	Flying sprints: 4-6 repetitions per session.
•	Sprint drills: 3 sessions per week.
•	Plyometric exercises: 3 sessions per week.
•	Speed endurance training: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Excessive tension in the body.
•	Poor posture.
•	Overstriding.
•	Inconsistent arm movement.
Career Guidance:
Elite maximum speed development is essential for athletes aiming to compete at regional, national, and international sprinting levels.
