RUNNING TECHNIQUE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Running technique is the foundation of sprint performance. Efficient sprint mechanics improve speed, reduce energy waste, and lower injury risk.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper sprint posture.
•	Practice coordinated arm movements.
•	Focus on correct foot placement.
•	Develop balance and rhythm.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve stride efficiency.
•	Enhance arm-leg coordination.
•	Increase running economy.
•	Develop consistent sprint mechanics.
Advanced Level:
•	Refine race-specific technique.
•	Optimize stride length and frequency.
•	Maintain efficiency at maximum speed.
•	Adapt technique to different race situations.
Training Recommendations:
•	Technique drills: 4 sessions per week.
•	A-skips and B-skips: 10-15 minutes per session.
•	Video analysis: Weekly review.
•	Mobility and flexibility training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor posture.
•	Excessive upper-body movement.
•	Inefficient stride patterns.
•	Incorrect foot placement.
Career Guidance:
Excellent running mechanics provide the foundation for long-term sprinting success and progression through school, club, regional, and elite competitions.
