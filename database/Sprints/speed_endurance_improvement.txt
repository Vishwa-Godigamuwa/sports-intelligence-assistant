SPEED ENDURANCE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Speed endurance is the ability to maintain high running speed throughout the race. It is particularly important in the 200m event and the final stages of the 100m.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn pacing awareness.
•	Practice maintaining form during fatigue.
•	Develop cardiovascular fitness.
•	Improve muscular endurance.
Intermediate Level:
•	Increase sprint distance tolerance.
•	Improve recovery between repetitions.
•	Develop consistent race pace.
•	Maintain technique at higher speeds.
Advanced Level:
•	Sustain near-maximum speed longer.
•	Resist fatigue effectively.
•	Optimize race-specific endurance.
•	Execute strong finishes.
Training Recommendations:
•	Interval sprints: 2-3 sessions per week.
•	150m-300m repeats: 4-8 repetitions per session.
•	Endurance running: 2 sessions per week.
•	Recovery sessions: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Starting too fast.
•	Losing form under fatigue.
•	Insufficient recovery.
•	Poor pacing strategy.
Career Guidance:
Strong speed endurance helps sprinters maintain competitive performance and improve race results.
