CRICKET BATTING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE

Overview:
Batting is the skill of scoring runs while protecting your wicket. A good batsman combines technique, concentration, and physical fitness.

Beginner Level:
- Learn the correct batting stance.
- Keep your eyes on the ball.
- Practice front-foot and back-foot defence.
- Focus on timing rather than power.

Intermediate Level:
- Improve footwork against spin and pace bowling.
- Practice cover drives, pull shots, and cut shots.
- Develop shot selection skills.
- Learn to rotate strike regularly.

Advanced Level:
- Analyse bowlers' strategies.
- Improve scoring in different match situations.
- Build consistency under pressure.
- Practice against varied bowling styles.

Training Recommendations:
- Net practice: 4-5 sessions per week.
- Throw-down drills: 100 balls per session.
- Shadow batting: 15 minutes daily.
- Fitness training: 3 sessions per week.

Common Mistakes:
- Swinging too hard.
- Poor foot movement.
- Looking away from the ball.
- Playing unnecessary shots.

Career Guidance:
Continuous practice and participation in school, club, and regional tournaments can help advance a batting career.
