GOLF PUTTING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Putting is the skill of rolling the ball into the hole on the green. Strong putting is one of the most important aspects of scoring success in golf.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper putting grip.
•	Focus on a smooth stroke.
•	Practice short putts.
•	Develop alignment awareness.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve distance control.
•	Practice reading simple greens.
•	Develop consistency from mid-range distances.
•	Improve confidence.
Advanced Level:
•	Master green reading techniques.
•	Control pace effectively.
•	Perform under pressure.
•	Reduce three-putts consistently.
Training Recommendations:
•	Putting drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Short putt practice: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Distance control drills: 20 minutes per session.
•	Green reading exercises: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor alignment.
•	Inconsistent stroke tempo.
•	Looking up too early.
•	Misjudging speed.
Career Guidance:
Excellent putting skills can dramatically improve scores and help golfers compete more effectively.
