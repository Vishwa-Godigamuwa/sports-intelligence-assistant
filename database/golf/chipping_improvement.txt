GOLF CHIPPING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Chipping is a short-game skill used near the green to position the ball close to the hole. Effective chipping helps players save strokes and improve scoring.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn basic chipping technique.
•	Focus on consistent contact.
•	Practice short-distance chips.
•	Develop touch and feel.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve distance control.
•	Practice different chip trajectories.
•	Learn to handle various lies.
•	Increase accuracy.
Advanced Level:
•	Master specialized chip shots.
•	Adapt to challenging course conditions.
•	Improve decision-making around the green.
•	Perform consistently under pressure.
Training Recommendations:
•	Chipping practice: 4 sessions per week.
•	Short-game drills: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Target practice: 20 minutes per session.
•	Match simulation exercises: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Excessive wrist movement.
•	Poor club selection.
•	Inconsistent contact.
•	Misjudging distance.
Career Guidance:
Strong chipping skills can significantly lower scores and improve competitive performance.
