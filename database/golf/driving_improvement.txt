GOLF DRIVING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Driving is the skill of hitting the ball long distances from the tee. Effective driving combines power, accuracy, and consistency to establish strong positions on the course.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper grip and stance.
•	Focus on balanced swings.
•	Develop consistent ball contact.
•	Prioritize accuracy over distance.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve swing mechanics.
•	Increase driving distance.
•	Develop consistent shot shape.
•	Improve tee-shot accuracy.
Advanced Level:
•	Control distance and trajectory.
•	Adapt to different course conditions.
•	Optimize driving strategy.
•	Maintain consistency under pressure.
Training Recommendations:
•	Driving range practice: 4 sessions per week.
•	Swing drills: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Flexibility exercises: 15 minutes daily.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Overswinging.
•	Poor balance.
•	Incorrect grip pressure.
•	Focusing only on distance.
Career Guidance:
Strong driving ability provides a competitive advantage and supports advancement in competitive golf.
