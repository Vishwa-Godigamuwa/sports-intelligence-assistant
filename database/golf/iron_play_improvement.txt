GOLF IRON PLAY IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Iron play involves hitting accurate approach shots toward the green. Effective iron play is essential for creating birdie opportunities and reducing scores.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper ball positioning.
•	Focus on clean ball contact.
•	Practice consistent swings.
•	Develop basic distance control.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve shot accuracy.
•	Learn to hit different trajectories.
•	Develop confidence with multiple clubs.
•	Improve distance consistency.
Advanced Level:
•	Shape shots intentionally.
•	Control spin and trajectory.
•	Adapt to varying course conditions.
•	Execute accurate approach shots under pressure.
Training Recommendations:
•	Iron practice: 4 sessions per week.
•	Target drills: 50 shots per session.
•	Distance control exercises: 3 sessions per week.
•	Strength training: 2-3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor alignment.
•	Inconsistent contact.
•	Rushing swings.
•	Lack of follow-through.
Career Guidance:
Strong iron play significantly improves scoring potential and overall golf performance.
