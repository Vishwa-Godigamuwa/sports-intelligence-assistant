GOLF COURSE MANAGEMENT IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Course management is the ability to make smart strategic decisions throughout a round. Good course management helps golfers minimize mistakes and maximize scoring opportunities.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn basic course strategy.
•	Choose safe targets.
•	Focus on consistent decision-making.
•	Avoid unnecessary risks.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve club selection.
•	Consider hazards and course layout.
•	Develop pre-shot routines.
•	Manage risk and reward effectively.
Advanced Level:
•	Create strategic game plans.
•	Adapt decisions to course conditions.
•	Analyze strengths and weaknesses.
•	Maintain focus throughout the round.
Training Recommendations:
•	Practice rounds: 2-3 per week.
•	Course strategy review: 15 minutes after each round.
•	Mental training: 3 sessions per week.
•	Competitive play experience: Regular participation.
Common Mistakes:
•	Taking unnecessary risks.
•	Poor club selection.
•	Ignoring course conditions.
•	Emotional decision-making.
Career Guidance:
Strong course management skills complement technical abilities and contribute to long-term success in competitive golf.
