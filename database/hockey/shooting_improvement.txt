HOCKEY SHOOTING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Shooting is the skill of scoring goals by striking the ball accurately and powerfully toward the goal. Effective shooting combines technique, accuracy, and decision-making.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn basic shooting mechanics.
•	Focus on accuracy rather than power.
•	Practice proper body positioning.
•	Aim for different areas of the goal.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve shot power and consistency.
•	Practice shooting from various angles.
•	Learn drag flick and slap shot basics.
•	Develop finishing skills inside the shooting circle.
Advanced Level:
•	Master different shooting techniques.
•	Shoot effectively under pressure.
•	Improve shot selection.
•	Maintain accuracy in game situations.
Training Recommendations:
•	Shooting drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Target shooting: 50 shots per session.
•	Match simulation exercises: 2 sessions per week.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor body alignment.
•	Focusing only on power.
•	Rushing shots.
•	Weak follow-through.
Career Guidance:
Consistent goal-scoring ability can help players become key attacking contributors at higher levels of hockey.
