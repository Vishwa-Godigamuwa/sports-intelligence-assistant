BADMINTON SERVING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Serving starts every rally in badminton. A good serve helps players gain an early advantage and prevents opponents from attacking immediately.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper serving grip.
•	Practice short and accurate serves.
•	Focus on legal serving technique.
•	Develop consistent shuttle contact.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve serve placement.
•	Practice high and low serves.
•	Develop serving consistency.
•	Learn basic deception techniques.
Advanced Level:
•	Vary serve speed and placement.
•	Disguise serving intentions.
•	Target opponents' weaknesses.
•	Maintain precision under pressure.
Training Recommendations:
•	Serving drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Target practice: 50 serves per session.
•	Grip control exercises: 15 minutes daily.
•	Match simulation drills: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Inconsistent shuttle contact.
•	Poor serve placement.
•	Excessive power.
•	Predictable serving patterns.
Career Guidance:
Strong serving skills help players establish control and improve success at competitive levels.
