BADMINTON CLEAR IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
The clear is a defensive and attacking shot used to send the shuttle to the opponent's backcourt. Effective clears create space and disrupt opponents' positioning.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn basic overhead technique.
•	Focus on height and distance.
•	Practice correct shuttle contact.
•	Develop timing and coordination.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve clear consistency.
•	Practice forehand and backhand clears.
•	Add accuracy and depth.
•	Increase shot control.
Advanced Level:
•	Execute clears under pressure.
•	Vary trajectory and pace.
•	Use clears strategically during rallies.
•	Improve recovery after each shot.
Training Recommendations:
•	Clear drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Multi-shuttle training: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Shoulder strengthening exercises: 3 sessions per week.
•	Footwork drills: 15 minutes daily.
Common Mistakes:
•	Hitting too late.
•	Poor body rotation.
•	Weak follow-through.
•	Lack of balance.
Career Guidance:
Strong clear shots provide a solid foundation for advanced badminton strategies and competitive play.
