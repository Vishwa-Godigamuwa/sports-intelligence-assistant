SWIMMING BUTTERFLY IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Butterfly is one of the most physically demanding swimming strokes. It requires strength, coordination, timing, and powerful body movement.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn the dolphin kick technique.
•	Practice basic arm recovery movements.
•	Focus on body rhythm.
•	Develop coordination between movements.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve timing between kick and arm action.
•	Increase stroke efficiency.
•	Develop breathing control.
•	Build strength and endurance.
Advanced Level:
•	Maximize power and speed.
•	Refine race strategy.
•	Improve technique under fatigue.
•	Maintain consistent stroke rhythm.
Training Recommendations:
•	Butterfly drills: 3-4 sessions per week.
•	Dolphin kick practice: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
•	Endurance swimming: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Overusing upper body strength.
•	Poor body rhythm.
•	Excessive breathing movements.
•	Weak dolphin kicks.
Career Guidance:
Butterfly specialists are highly respected for their technical ability and physical conditioning in competitive swimming.
