SWIMMING BACKSTROKE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Backstroke is performed while swimming on the back, requiring good body alignment, coordinated arm movements, and strong kicking technique.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper floating position.
•	Practice basic arm recovery movements.
•	Develop a steady flutter kick.
•	Focus on straight-line swimming.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve stroke coordination.
•	Increase stroke efficiency.
•	Practice turns and wall approaches.
•	Develop consistent pacing.
Advanced Level:
•	Optimize stroke rate and timing.
•	Improve speed and endurance.
•	Master backstroke starts and turns.
•	Maintain technique during race conditions.
Training Recommendations:
•	Backstroke sessions: 4 times per week.
•	Technique drills: 20 minutes per session.
•	Kick training: 15 minutes daily.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Sinking hips.
•	Uneven arm movements.
•	Poor body alignment.
•	Excessive head movement.
Career Guidance:
Backstroke specialists can compete successfully in individual backstroke and medley competitions at various levels.
