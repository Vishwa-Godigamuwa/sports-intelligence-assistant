SWIMMING FREESTYLE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Freestyle is the fastest and most commonly used swimming stroke. It combines efficient arm movements, strong kicking, and proper breathing techniques to maximize speed and endurance.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper body position in the water.
•	Practice basic arm movements.
•	Develop a consistent flutter kick.
•	Focus on comfortable breathing techniques.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve stroke efficiency.
•	Practice bilateral breathing.
•	Increase swimming distance without fatigue.
•	Develop better coordination between arms and legs.
Advanced Level:
•	Maximize speed and endurance.
•	Improve stroke rate and timing.
•	Maintain technique during intense training.
•	Adapt pacing strategies for different race distances.
Training Recommendations:
•	Freestyle practice: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Technique drills: 20 minutes per session.
•	Endurance swimming: 30-45 minutes per session.
•	Strength and conditioning: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Lifting the head too high when breathing.
•	Poor body alignment.
•	Overkicking.
•	Short and inefficient arm strokes.
Career Guidance:
Freestyle is the foundation of competitive swimming and is essential for success in most swimming events and competitions.
