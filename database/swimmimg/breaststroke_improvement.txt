SWIMMING BREASTSTROKE IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Breaststroke is a technique-based swimming stroke that requires precise timing between arm movements, leg kicks, and breathing for maximum efficiency.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn the basic breaststroke kick.
•	Practice arm and leg coordination.
•	Focus on smooth movement through the water.
•	Develop proper breathing timing.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve stroke timing.
•	Increase glide efficiency.
•	Enhance kick power.
•	Maintain rhythm throughout longer swims.
Advanced Level:
•	Maximize propulsion while reducing drag.
•	Improve race pacing.
•	Refine start and turn techniques.
•	Maintain efficiency under fatigue.
Training Recommendations:
•	Breaststroke drills: 4 sessions per week.
•	Kick drills: 50 repetitions per session.
•	Technique practice: 20 minutes per session.
•	Endurance training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor kick technique.
•	Rushing the stroke cycle.
•	Excessive head movement.
•	Reducing glide time.
Career Guidance:
Strong breaststroke skills can help swimmers compete successfully in individual and medley events at advanced levels.
