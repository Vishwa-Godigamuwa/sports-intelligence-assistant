TENNIS BACKHAND IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
The backhand is an essential stroke used when the ball is played to the non-dominant side. A strong backhand improves consistency and allows players to handle pressure effectively.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn basic one-handed or two-handed backhand technique.
•	Focus on balance and timing.
•	Practice controlled strokes.
•	Keep your eyes on the ball.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve shot consistency.
•	Develop topspin and directional control.
•	Practice cross-court and down-the-line shots.
•	Improve movement to the ball.
Advanced Level:
•	Attack short balls confidently.
•	Handle high-speed shots effectively.
•	Vary spin and pace.
•	Maintain consistency in long rallies.
Training Recommendations:
•	Backhand drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Wall practice: 100 strokes per session.
•	Footwork training: 15 minutes daily.
•	Strength training: 3 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Poor racket preparation.
•	Weak body rotation.
•	Hitting too late.
•	Lack of balance.
Career Guidance:
A dependable backhand can make players more versatile and competitive at higher levels
