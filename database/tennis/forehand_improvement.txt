TENNIS FOREHAND IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
The forehand is often the primary attacking shot in tennis. A strong forehand allows players to control rallies, create opportunities, and win points effectively.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn proper forehand grip.
•	Focus on clean ball contact.
•	Develop a smooth swing path.
•	Practice basic rallying.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve consistency and accuracy.
•	Generate more topspin.
•	Practice hitting on the move.
•	Develop directional control.
Advanced Level:
•	Master powerful attacking forehands.
•	Use forehands to control court positioning.
•	Adapt shot selection to match situations.
•	Maintain consistency under pressure.
Training Recommendations:
•	Forehand drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Rally practice: 100 strokes per session.
•	Footwork exercises: 15 minutes daily.
•	Match simulation drills: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Swinging too hard.
•	Poor foot positioning.
•	Late ball contact.
•	Inconsistent follow-through.
Career Guidance:
A reliable forehand is a key weapon for competitive tennis players and can significantly improve match performance.
