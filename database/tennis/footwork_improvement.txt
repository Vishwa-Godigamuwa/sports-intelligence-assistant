TENNIS FOOTWORK IMPROVEMENT GUIDE
Overview:
Footwork is the foundation of tennis performance. Good movement enables players to reach the ball quickly, maintain balance, and execute shots effectively.
Beginner Level:
•	Learn basic movement patterns.
•	Practice split-step technique.
•	Maintain balance while moving.
•	Develop court awareness.
Intermediate Level:
•	Improve lateral movement.
•	Recover quickly after each shot.
•	Increase speed and agility.
•	Practice movement under match conditions.
Advanced Level:
•	Anticipate opponents' shots.
•	Optimize court coverage.
•	Maintain movement efficiency during long matches.
•	Combine footwork with tactical play.
Training Recommendations:
•	Footwork drills: 4-5 sessions per week.
•	Agility ladder exercises: 15-20 minutes daily.
•	Sprint training: 3 sessions per week.
•	Match simulation exercises: 2 sessions per week.
Common Mistakes:
•	Standing flat-footed.
•	Slow recovery after shots.
•	Poor balance.
•	Inefficient movement patterns.
Career Guidance:
Excellent footwork enhances all aspects of tennis performance and supports long-term competitive development.
