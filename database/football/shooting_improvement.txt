FOOTBALL SHOOTING IMPROVEMENT GUIDE

Overview:
Shooting is the ability to accurately and powerfully direct the ball towards the goal.

Beginner Level:
- Learn proper foot placement.
- Focus on ball contact.
- Practice basic stationary shots.
- Aim for accuracy before power.

Intermediate Level:
- Shoot while moving.
- Practice both feet.
- Improve finishing inside the penalty area.
- Learn different shooting techniques.

Advanced Level:
- Improve decision making under pressure.
- Master long-range shots.
- Develop first-time finishing skills.
- Train with defenders present.

Training Recommendations:
- 50-100 shots per training session.
- Target corner finishing drills.
- One-on-one shooting scenarios.
- Small-sided games.

Common Mistakes:
- Leaning back excessively.
- Looking at the goal too early.
- Rushing the shot.
- Using only the dominant foot.

Career Guidance:
Regular participation in leagues and competitive matches can improve visibility to coaches and scouts.
